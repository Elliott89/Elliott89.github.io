Privacy Policy

Samwise Calendar is a private tool used only by its owner's household.

What it accesses: events and the calendar list on the owner's Google Calendar.

How it is used: to show the household's upcoming events and, only after the owner approves, to add or change events.

Sharing: the data is not sold or shared with anyone. It stays on the owner's own computer and the services the owner uses to run the assistant.

Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
